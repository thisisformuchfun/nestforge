## Reading the index

- **Print ZIP** contains the current STL/3MF print package. **STEP ZIP** is the separate structural CAD download. Use GitHub’s **Download raw file** action when viewing a ZIP.
- **CAD envelope** is the X × Y × Z extent calculated from the assembly-coordinate part bounds, rounded to 0.1 mm. It is not a printer bed size or a measurement of a physical print, and excludes purchased equipment.
- **Colors** counts the complete design palette. Every plate stays within four AMS slots; a build can require a different color load on another plate.
- Click a design name to open its live detail view. The complete release contains its folder with the preview, `PRINT-GUIDE.html`, model metadata and stored checks.

## Special model notes

**Dragon Guard Citadel** uses the attributed Artec Fantasy Dragon sculpt. The full printable sculpt is supplied in its meshes and 3MF; its STEP contains the mechanical structure. Keep the [license and modification notes](../cad-source/cad/COMMUNITY-ASSETS.md) with any derivative.

**Sentry Cam Feeder** belongs to the original Future habitats family and is in the current camera-free group. Its name does not make it one of the ten R7 `camera_system` designs. Use the design metadata rather than the name to determine its current camera requirements.

The [R1 archive](https://github.com/thisisformuchfun/nestforge/releases/tag/v1.0.0) preserves 28 earlier feeder packages, and [R2](https://github.com/thisisformuchfun/nestforge/releases/tag/v1.0.0) preserves the 37-design collection at that stage. Their counts and geometry should not be added to the current 47 as if they were new designs.

The retained [R6 guide and source](../cad-source/cad/R6-GUIDE.md) describe twelve historical Vegas-inspired camera concepts: Bellagio, Venetian, Caesars, Paris, Excalibur, New York-New York, Luxor, Flamingo, Wynn, legacy Mirage, Strat and Circus Circus. These generators are historical source and are not the active R7 camera downloads. No affiliation with the hotels is implied.
